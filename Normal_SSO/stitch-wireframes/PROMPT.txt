Use the attached image as the layout reference. Follow its structure, proportions and colours closely. Redraw it as a polished, high-fidelity UI.

CONTEXT
A web page used by Malaysian primary school pupils aged 7 to 9 in a school computer lab. They choose their class, tap their name, then choose a destination. Everything is in Bahasa Melayu. Users may not read fluently and have poor mouse control.

The school has 6 tahun (year groups), each with 8 or 9 classes — up to 54 classes — and 30 to 44 pupils per class.

STYLE
Warm, calm, playful — like a page from a children's picture book. Large friendly shapes, flat colour, generous white space, one clear idea per screen. NOT a dashboard, NOT a corporate portal, NOT a SaaS admin panel. No gradients on text, no glassmorphism, no dark mode, no sidebars, no breadcrumbs, no stat tiles, no data tables.

Rounded friendly sans-serif (Nunito, Quicksand or Baloo 2). Corner radius 20px on large cards, 16px on small cards and buttons. Soft shadow 0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08). Spacing scale 8/16/24/32/48px.

COLOUR — from the SK Seksyen 24 Shah Alam school crest. Use these exact values.
  Page background   #FFFDF7
  Cards / surface   #FFFFFF
  Soft surface      #FDF9DC
  Primary text      #1A1208
  Secondary text    #5C5344
  Border            #E5DCC8
  Brand green       #056839  — white text on it
  Brand orange      #F7941D  — DARK #1A1208 text on it, NEVER white
  Brand red         #ED1B24  — thin rules and decoration only, NEVER text
  Class colours: #C41118 #9E2B0E #A85200 #8A6100 #056839 #2F6B12 #0A6265 #7A4A21
  All class-coloured surfaces use WHITE text. Do not lighten, tint or add opacity.

Never place white text on #F7941D — it is unreadable at 2.3:1. The school crest itself puts black lettering on its orange ribbon; follow that. Never place text of any colour on #ED1B24.

No blues, no purples, no cool greys anywhere. Greys must be warm.

TYPOGRAPHY
  Screen heading   40px bold
  Class name       28px bold
  Pupil name       18px bold
  Button label     28px bold
  Helper text      16px regular
Nothing below 16px. No ALL CAPS. Line height 1.4 minimum. Never truncate a name with an ellipsis — wrap to two lines.

TOUCH TARGETS — non-negotiable
Every interactive element at least 48x48px. These users are 7 years old with unsteady hands.
Two card sizes exist and they are very different — do not average them:
  - Class-level cards, dropdowns and buttons: large, 240x180px or bigger, 24px gaps.
  - Pupil name cards on screen 2: SMALL, 137-179px wide by 99px tall with 10px gaps, because a whole class must fit one screen. Rows are always 5; the column count follows the class size, so card height is constant and only width changes.

SCREEN-SPECIFIC NOTES

Screen 1 — Landing page: HEADER, then TWO COLUMNS with a 48px gutter, then a FOOTER.
HEADER, full width, 88px tall, white, closed by a 3px #ED1B24 rule. On the left the school crest at 56px, then a two-line lockup — "SK Seksyen 24 Shah Alam" at 20px bold with the school motto "Berilmu Berdisiplin" below it at 14px in warm grey. On the right a small BM/EN segmented language toggle and an outlined "Guru" button, both 48px tall.
LEFT (about 45%) is the form: heading "Pilih Kelas Anda"; a "Kelas Terakhir" shortcut card; a "Tahun" dropdown listing Tahun 1 to Tahun 6; a "Kelas" dropdown shown DISABLED with placeholder "Pilih tahun dahulu"; and a full-width green "Teruskan →" button 64px tall.
The Kelas list is variable-length — some tahun have 8 classes, some 9 — so when open and longer than 8 rows the panel scrolls internally rather than growing off the bottom of the screen.
Both dropdowns are large CUSTOM controls, not native browser selects — 72px tall closed, and when open their rows are 64px tall with 20px text and a round colour swatch. Never draw a small OS-style select popup.
RIGHT (about 55%) is an information panel for ADULTS — teachers, parents, administrators. A #FDF9DC rounded panel with 40px padding, heading "Apa itu laman ini?", one line of body text, three numbered steps with orange circular badges, a reassurance block on white with a green left bar stating that no passwords are stored, and the two destination logos. This column contains NO controls.
There are NO class cards on this screen — classes come from the dropdowns.
FOOTER, full width, 64px, very quiet, separated by a 1px #E5DCC8 line: "Senarai dikemas kini 6 Ogos 2026" on the left in 14px grey, "Masalah? Panggil cikgu." on the right in 14px grey. No buttons, no links, nothing tappable.

Screen 2 — "Cari Nama Anda"
Compact 80px top bar, NOT the tall landing header: "Kembali" button far left, class pill "Tahun 2 Cemerlang" centred, school crest at 36px far right. The tall header must not appear here — the card grid needs the vertical space.
A DENSE grid of pupil cards. Classes hold 30 to 44 pupils, and the whole class plus one extra card must fit one 1366x768 screen with NO SCROLLING — a hard requirement. Rows are always 5; the column count follows the class size.
Draw the largest case: 44 pupils plus one extra card = 45 items, 9 columns x 5 rows, each 137x99px with 10px gaps.
Each card is white, 16px radius, thin border, containing a small flat-illustration animal avatar at 40px in a soft circular #FDF9DC background, and the pupil's name below it at 18px bold, up to two lines.
At this width a card fits about 14 characters per line, so use short calling names: Aishah, Danial, Siti, Wei Ming, Arjun, Iman, Haziq, Mei Ling, Farhan, Zara, Amirul, Hana, Ravi, Syafiq, Nabila, and so on. Where two pupils share a calling name, disambiguate with an initial — "Nur Aishah A." and "Nur Aishah O." No two cards may read alike.
The FINAL card is different and must be drawn: an OUTLINED card (no fill, 2px dashed border) with a question-mark icon instead of an animal avatar and the label "Nama saya tiada". It is the escape hatch for a pupil whose name is missing from the list.
Avatars are simple bold flat shapes — animals like cat, rabbit, elephant, fish, bird, tiger, turtle, bee. No gradients, no photorealism, no 3D.
The search box is deliberately SECONDARY — thin border, no fill, no shadow. It is a tool for the teacher, not the pupil.
The grid should feel like a wall of laminated classroom name cards, calm and orderly — not a spreadsheet.

Screen 3 — "Pergi ke Mana?"
Same compact 80px top bar as screen 2, with the 36px crest on the right.
Exactly four elements: the pupil's avatar and FULL name, the masked email, two destination buttons, one small "Bukan saya" link. Nothing else.
This is the only screen showing the full name — it is the identity check before handoff.
The email is ALWAYS masked as m-1234••••@moe-dl.edu.my — never show a full email address anywhere in the interface.
The "DELIMa 3.0" button is #056839 with white text; the "Google Classroom" button is #F7941D with dark #1A1208 text. Both identical in size and weight — the colour difference is so a pre-reader can tell them apart, not to rank them.

ACCESSIBILITY
All text meets 4.5:1 contrast. Never use colour alone to carry meaning. Every card shows a clear focus ring in #056839.

LANGUAGE
All interface text in Bahasa Melayu. Never use the words SSO, portal, autentikasi, sesi, or log masuk tunggal. Back buttons are labelled "Kembali" with an arrow — never a bare arrow icon. Keep only "DELIMa 3.0" and "Google Classroom" untranslated.
